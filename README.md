# In-Vitro Evaluation of Hepatic Protective Potential of *Pergularia daemia* Extract on Liver Cells

## About the Project

This academic project evaluates the in-vitro hepatic protective potential of *Pergularia daemia* extract on liver cells. The study focuses on plant extraction, phytochemical screening, steroid detection, and evaluation of hepatoprotective activity.

## Objectives

- To prepare *Pergularia daemia* plant extract.
- To perform phytochemical screening.
- To detect the presence of steroid compounds.
- To evaluate the hepatic protective potential of the plant extract.
- To analyze the experimental results.

## Methodology

1. Plant sample collection
2. Sample preparation
3. Plant extraction
4. Phytochemical screening
5. Steroid detection
6. Hepatoprotective activity evaluation
7. Statistical analysis

## My Contribution

I was involved in sample testing and confirmatory testing for the presence of steroid compounds.
